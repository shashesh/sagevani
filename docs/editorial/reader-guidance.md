# Reader guidance

## Confirmed requirements

SageVani welcomes readers from novice to advanced. Blog pieces should carry a difficulty level. When a topic is advanced, provide suggested readings before it so readers can build the necessary context.

The difficulty system describes the background a piece expects, not the reader's spiritual attainment. English is the current publication language.

## Proposed labels and criteria — awaiting owner approval

| Level | Expected background | Support in the piece |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner | No prior study assumed | Define essential terms and provide necessary context. |
| Intermediate | Some familiarity with the relevant terms or text | Summarize assumed context and suggest introductory readings where useful. |
| Advanced | Familiarity with the texts, schools, or interpretive debates involved | State expected background and list suggested prior readings. |

Label choices and placement have not been approved. The future website should implement the confirmed reading guidance once its platform and design are selected.
