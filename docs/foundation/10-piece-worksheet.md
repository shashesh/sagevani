G. Integrity check

I can locate the quotations I use.

I am not presenting a school-specific claim as universal.

My symbolism is labeled if it is my own.

Sanskrit wording has been checked when the argument depends on it.

I am not using spirituality to minimize suffering or avoid action.

The article contains something I have genuinely had to look at in myself.

I would still stand behind the piece if it received little attention.

H. One sentence after the draft

What did writing this make me see that I did not see before?
