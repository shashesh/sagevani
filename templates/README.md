# Templates

Copy templates into the working directory; retain originals for reuse. Fields are prompts, not required public article headings. Metadata and record formats are proposed operational aids.

- [Seed card](seed-card.md) → Vault.
- [Source card](source-card.md) → research.
- [Story card](story-card.md) → research or Vault.
- [Piece worksheet](piece-worksheet.md) → `content/drafts/`.
- [Editorial review](editorial-review.md) → the article's editorial checklist in the CMS.
- [Publication record](publication-record.md) → publication records, after actual publication.

Templates adapt the handbook. Added verification, approval, and tracking fields are proposals, not approved amendments.

Article drafts and their reviews live in the website's CMS (D-005) and stay off the website until the owner publishes. Completed piece worksheets stay in `content/drafts/`, also off the website. "Private" here means unpublished, as clarified by the owner. Assistant drafting needs no separate permission; publication requires owner approval. Reading-level guidance is required by the owner; its label system remains proposed.
