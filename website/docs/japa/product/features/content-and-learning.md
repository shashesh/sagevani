# Content and learning

## Starter articles (P1)

Articles are SageVani articles, written in the website's CMS and linked from `/japa` ([D-006](../../../../../docs/governance/decisions.md#d-006--japadhyan-joins-sagevani-at-japa)). JapaDhyan has no article system of its own.

- What is naam japam / mantra sadhana?
- Benefits of name chanting — traditional teachings and what research says
- How to begin: time, place, posture, count
- How to use a mala
- Aloud, whispered, or silent — the three forms of japa
- What is a sankalpa?

## Growth (P2)

- Articles by tradition and sampradaya, and by deity.
- Stories and teachings of saints on the power of the Name.
- Content reviewed by a qualified advisor before publishing.

## Pronunciation

- Audio for every library mantra (P1), recorded by qualified reciters.
- [Chant along](chanting-modes.md#chant-along) mode (P2).

## Ambient sound (P2)

- Tanpura drone, temple bells, flowing water, or silence.
- Offline, low-volume, never interrupting counting.

## Format

- Short and readable on a phone. Articles are blog pages, so reading one needs a connection; `/japa` itself works offline.
- Never shown between opening the app and chanting ([vision](../vision.md)).
